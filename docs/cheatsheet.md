# VirtualGlove — Quick Reference

Your hand is the controller. Use this page to get ready, play, and get back into a game when something goes wrong.

## Open VirtualGlove

Open `http://YOUR-UNO-Q.local/` on your phone or computer and choose **VirtualGlove**. If it is the only installed app, it opens automatically. You can also bookmark `http://YOUR-UNO-Q.local:8100/dashboard`.

Use your UNO Q's name in place of `YOUR-UNO-Q`. If its name does not open, use the IP address printed by the installer or shown by your network router.

| Page | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | See your hand, centre it, and start controller output. |
| **Play** | Play Rock Paper Scissors in your browser. |
| **Learn** | Practise gestures in Glove Academy. |
| **Setup** | Pair your console, choose a camera, and edit game filenames. |
| **Apps** | Open the app chooser or Controller Router Setup. |
| **Help** | Read the guides and find troubleshooting steps. |

## Before you play

1. Connect the camera and point it at your playing area.
2. Open **Dashboard** and choose your player.
3. Hold your hand comfortably in view. Choose **Centre hand** if this is your first session or your camera or playing position changed.
4. Choose **Start controller**.
5. Launch a registered game on your console. Its game profile activates automatically; you do not need to keep the website open.
6. Rest your hand in the centre once, then try a movement or gesture.

Close local **Play** or **Glove Academy** before playing on the console; these activities pause console input.

## Pick the right controls

| You want to… | What to use |
| --- | --- |
| Play with direction and button gestures | A joystick profile for your game. |
| Play Super Glove Ball with hand movement | **Nestopia (VirtualGlove)** on your console. |
| Practise before starting a game | **Learn > Glove Academy**. |
| Check the gestures for a particular game | The [Game and Gesture Guide](GAMEPLAY_GUIDE.md). |
| Exit a console game | Your physical Player 1 controller's configured hotkey combination. |

The glove's Select gesture is a game control. It is not the console's exit hotkey.

## Choose players and systems

Open **Apps**, then **Setup** in Controller Router. Select your console.

1. Under **Players**, assign each pad to its player. Keep a physical pad on Player 1 for menus and exit controls.
2. Under **Systems**, choose **Controller Router** or **My existing setup**.
3. Close the game and choose **Save assignments**. The choices apply next time you launch a game.

**Buddy’s games and VirtualGlove require Controller Router enabled for NES.** Other systems can keep your existing controls. Installation and upgrades preserve your saved choices.

## Quick recovery

| What you see | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| No hand detected | Check the camera, lighting, and that your full hand is visible. |
| Hand detected, but no movement | Choose **Start controller**, check the game filename, then rest your hand in the centre. |
| The wrong pad responds | Try another pad; check **Router Setup > Players**. |
| A wireless pad sleeps or wakes | Wake it. If Router itself restarted, close and relaunch the game. |
| The console is not connected | Check its address in **Setup** and complete pairing if requested. |
| A gesture is difficult to recognise | Practise it in **Learn** before changing its settings. |
