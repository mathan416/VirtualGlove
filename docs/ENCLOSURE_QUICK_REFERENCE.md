# VirtualGlove Enclosure Assembly Quick Reference

Use this visual checklist after the enclosure parts have been printed and test
fitted. For printer settings, tolerances, heat-set insert technique, downloads,
and troubleshooting, use the [Controller Enclosure Guide](ENCLOSURE_GUIDE.md).

## UNO Q Case

![Exploded assembly view of the UNO Q Case](../hardware/enclosures/previews/virtualglove-uno-case-exploded.png)

You need the UNO Q, printed base and lid, Matrix bezel, hand/target emblem,
four M3 heat-set inserts, four M3 screws, a heat-set tool, and an M3 driver.

1. **Fit the inserts.** Heat each insert squarely and stop when it is flush.
2. **Mount the UNO Q.** The USB-C socket faces the broad side opening.
3. **Close the case.** Lower the lid without forcing it, then install the four
   case screws.
4. **Finish and check.** Fit the bezel and emblem. Confirm that the Matrix and
   USB-C opening remain clear.

## Controller Dock

![Exploded assembly view of the Controller Dock](../hardware/enclosures/previews/virtualglove-controller-dock-exploded.png)

You need the UNO Q, Arduino USB-C Hub, printed Dock base and lid, Matrix bezel,
hand/target emblem, four M3 heat-set inserts, four M3 screws, a heat-set tool,
and an M3 driver.

1. **Mount the UNO Q.** Fit the inserts first. The USB-C socket faces the broad
   opening.
2. **Seat and connect the hub.** Keep its ports facing outward and route its
   captive USB-C lead through the channel to the UNO Q.
3. **Close the Dock.** Make sure the cable is not pinched, then install the four
   case screws.
4. **Finish and check.** Fit the bezel and emblem. Confirm that the Matrix,
   UNO Q USB-C opening, hub ports, and ventilation remain clear.

## Before power

- Disconnect power before opening either enclosure.
- Do not trap or sharply bend the hub cable under the lid.
- Keep every vent and hub port unobstructed.
- Stop if the lid needs force; inspect the board, cable, and inserts first.
