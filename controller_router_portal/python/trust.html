First time on this device?

Allow your phone or computer to recognise this controller. You only need to do this once on each device.

First visit on iPhone or iPad

  1. Set up iPhone or iPad and choose Allow when Safari asks to download a configuration profile.
  2. Open Settings > Profile Downloaded. Choose the Controller Router Secure Setup profile and tap Install. If Profile Downloaded is not visible, open Settings > General > VPN & Device Management.
  3. Open Settings > General > About > Certificate Trust Settings. Turn on full trust for this controller’s Controller Router certificate and confirm.
  4. Return here and choose Continue to pairing.

This lets your iPhone or iPad recognise the controller’s secure pairing page.

First visit on a Mac

  1. Set up this Mac and open the downloaded file to add it to Keychain Access.
  2. In Keychain Access, open the Controller Router entry.
  3. Expand Trust, set Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) to Always Trust, and close the certificate window. macOS may ask you to authorize the change.
  4. Quit and reopen Safari, then choose Continue to pairing above.

Other devices and browsers

Accept or import the local certificate

Choose Continue to pairing. If your browser asks you to approve the controller’s secure page, follow its instructions. If it does not offer a way to continue, download the public certificate and import it into your device’s trusted certificate settings. Connection details below provide the information needed to verify the certificate. Return here and continue to pairing after completing setup.
