Get ready to play

Controller Router connects to your console and puts each gamepad with the player you choose. Pick a task below to get started or fix a problem.

Connect a consoleChoose playersChoose systemsTest a controllerFix a problem

Connect a console

  1. Open Pair console. Run the command shown there on your RetroPie, Batocera, or Recalbox console.
  2. Paste the console’s connection code and choose Continue.
  3. Enter the six digits shown after PN on the Matrix display, then choose Connect.

Pair your console once. If both VirtualGlove and R.O.B. Vision are installed, they can use the same connection.

More detail: Pairing Guide (PDF).

Choose who plays

  1. Set up your gamepad’s buttons in EmulationStation first.
  2. Exit the game. Open Players and Systems, choose your console, and find the pad under Players.
  3. Choose a player, select Save assignments, and launch the game again.

Keep a physical pad on Player 1 for the usual game and exit controls. Buddy uses Player 2 in Gyromite.

Choose which systems use Router

  1. Exit the game. Open Players and Systems and go to Systems.
  2. Choose Controller Router for systems that should use your player assignments, or My existing setup to keep their usual controls.
  3. Choose Save assignments. Your choice takes effect with the next game.

Buddy’s games and VirtualGlove require Controller Router enabled for NES.

Test a controller

  1. Open Players and Systems and choose the console.
  2. Choose Test inputs, then press a button on the gamepad within five seconds.
  3. If no input appears, wake or reconnect the pad and try again.

A registered game selects VirtualGlove or R.O.B. Vision automatically. You do not need to leave a browser page open to play.

More detail: Controller Router User Guide (PDF).

Get back to your game

The pad works in the menu but not in the game

Try another connected pad. Your first pad may be assigned to Player 2. Exit the game, check Players, then save and relaunch.

A wireless pad slept or woke during play

Wake or reconnect it, then try again. If Router itself restarted, exit and relaunch the game.

Assignments will not save

Finish the current game. If another page changed the settings, choose Reload, make your choices again, and save.

The console says unavailable

Check that it is powered on and on the same network. Then open Pair console and choose Check and repair connections.
